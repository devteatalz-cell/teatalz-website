Safety

How we look after the people who use what we build

These are not features of one product. They are the rules every product of ours has to pass before it ships.

🤝

Nobody is left alone

Every message a person sends is checked for signs that they may be in distress, and verified helpline information is shown when it is needed. If that check cannot run, the product does not answer at all — it fails towards care, never past it.

🪪

We never pretend

Our AI says that it is an AI. No guru name, no invented human behind it, no suggestion that a real person is reading. We would rather be trusted than believed.

🔇

No fear, no ads

Nothing we build frightens a person into spending. There are no ads, so we never earn by holding attention, and nothing we say is shaped by what it would sell.

🧭

We never guess

Where the software genuinely does not know something, it withholds instead of guessing. A confident answer that was invented is worse than no answer, especially for someone already worried.

🗝️

Your data stays yours

You can see what we hold, export it, and delete your account from inside the product — no email, no waiting, no persuasion on the way out.

👶

Not built for children

We set honest age ratings and we do not design anything to be compelling to a child. Our products are for adults with adult questions.

Be first through the door

One note when Ask Ru opens on Google Play, and one when Teatalz follows. Nothing else, ever.

🔒 Your details are safe — encrypted, never sold, and no spam. Just a warm welcome and occasional updates you can opt out of anytime.
