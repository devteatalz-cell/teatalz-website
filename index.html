Good to know

Questions, answered

The honest basics about Teatalz, Rume, and your privacy.

Is Teatalz free to use?

Yes — Teatalz is free to start. On the free plan you can chat with Rume, use Sukoon (gentle emotional support, always free), and join Adda. Sukoon draws on the same usage as the rest of Rume, and when a conversation gets heavy we add a little more room rather than cut it short. Paid plans (College ₹99, Lite ₹299, Pro ₹599/mo) unlock more usage, voice, and extra modes when you want them.

Is Teatalz a therapy app?

No. Rume is an emotional-support companion, not a therapist or a crisis service. When a conversation signals real risk, Teatalz surfaces verified helplines like India's Tele-MANAS (14416). For clinical needs, please reach a qualified professional — we'll always be honest about that.

What happens to my chats?

Your conversations are yours. They're encrypted, readable only by you, and auto-delete after 7 days. You can export or permanently delete everything, anytime.

Is my data private?

Yes — privacy is the architecture, not a setting. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, locked down at the database level so only you can read your conversations, and never sold. Teatalz is built to be DPDP-compliant.

Why do posts disappear?

On Adda, posts gently vanish after 7 days. No permanent record means people can speak freely and honestly, without it following them around. Kindness and impermanence, by design.

Why is Adda different?

Adda is a social space engineered for warmth instead of outrage — anonymous-capable, gentle, ephemeral, and ad-free. Built for support and belonging, not comparison and conflict.
