About Teatalz House Of Rume

We build AI products that refuse to do the easy thing.

Teatalz House Of Rume Private Limited builds software for the moments people would rather not discuss with a stranger. Our first product answers questions. Our second, when it returns, gives people somewhere to say the thing out loud.

What we build

  • Ask Ru — launching, Android. One question, answered from your birth chart, from tarot cards you pick yourself, or from a single Oracle card. ₹25 buys the answer and the forty follow-ups after it, with no clock running.
  • Teatalz — returns in 2027. The social network we are named after: Adda, a feed built for warmth instead of outrage, with Ru as a private space inside it. Every post read before anyone sees it. We set it aside deliberately to finish Ask Ru properly; it is not cancelled.

What we refuse

Most of what distinguishes our products is not something we added. It is something we refused, usually at a cost.

  • No meter. Everywhere else in this category, money is tied to time, so a worried person shortens the question and leaves before saying the real thing. We price the answer, not the minutes, and nothing auto-renews.
  • Nothing sold out of fear. A remedy from Ru is something you do — a manifestation, a pooja you perform yourself, a fast, a mantra, a donation, feeding animals. Never a gemstone, a bracelet, or anything you have to buy. Selling those objects is the fastest-growing line in this category, and it is closed to us by choice.
  • We withhold rather than guess. A birth chart is computed before our AI is allowed to speak, and it cannot state a position the chart does not contain. If you do not know your time of birth, we leave out the parts that depend on it instead of quietly assuming one.
  • Aligned with you, not advertisers. No ads. We earn from serving you well, never from selling your attention.
An honest note: Ru is an AI, and she says so — no guru name, no human backstory. She is not a therapist, a doctor or a crisis service, and she will not tell you what will happen to you or to anyone else. Every message is checked for signs of distress, and verified helplines are shown when they are needed. Setting that expectation honestly is core to who we are.
