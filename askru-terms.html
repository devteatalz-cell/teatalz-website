1. What Ask Ru is — and what it is not

Ask Ru gives you readings: from your birth chart, from tarot cards you pick yourself, or from a single Oracle card. Ru is an AI. She is not a person and does not pretend to be one.

Ask Ru is for reflection and guidance. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis or a prognosis; not legal advice; not financial or investment advice; and not a prediction of what will happen to you or to anyone else.

Ru will not name a disease, promise a cure or a recovery, or tell you whether anyone will die, fall ill or recover. She will not tell you what another person will do or feel. If you are worried about your health, see a doctor. If you are in distress, the app will show you helpline information — please use it.

2. Remedies: things you do, never things you buy

Ru may suggest a remedy — a manifestation, a pooja you perform yourself, a fast, a mantra, a donation, feeding animals, or something small that helps the living world around you.

Ru will never suggest a gemstone, a bracelet, a stone, a yantra, a thread, or anything else that has to be bought — not from us, not from anyone. She will never tell you to pay someone to perform a ritual on your behalf. If you are ever asked to buy something to fix a reading, it is not us.

3. Your account

You sign in with your email address and a code we send to it. Keep access to that email address safe; anyone who has it can reach your account. One account per person.

4. What you pay

Today, Ask Ru gives you one free reading a week, which you may use on any of the three sections.

When paid readings are available in the app, these terms apply:

  • A reading costs a fixed price, shown to you before you ask. That one price covers the reading and the follow-up questions on it — there is no clock, and asking more does not cost more.

  • Money in your wallet does not expire.
  • There is no subscription, and nothing auto-renews. We will never set up a recurring charge on your account.
  • If Ru cannot give you a reading, that question is not charged. If it was charged, it goes back to your wallet.
