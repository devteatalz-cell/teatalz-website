Three ways she reads you

One question at a time. Each one answers a different kind of question, and she will tell you if you have come to the wrong door.

Vedic

Your real birth chart, worked out properly — not a sun sign. For the questions that are about a stretch of your life rather than a day.

  • Lagna, Rasi, D9 and D10
  • Vimshottari periods, down to Prana when your birth time can carry it
  • Today's transits drawn on your own chart
  • Up to five more charts — read one alone, or beside yours

Tarot

One card for a straight question. Five when you want the shape of it — where it came from, what is in the way, and where it is going.

  • You shuffle. You pick.
  • Five positions: Past, Now, Block, Advice, Ahead
  • Ask her to clarify any one of them
  • Follow-ups on the same reading, not a fresh start

Oracle

For the days you cannot put the question into words yet. One card, one message, no spread to decode.

  • A single card, drawn with a breath
  • Its own deck — not the tarot in another coat
  • Said plainly, in a few short lines

How a reading goes

Three sections, three different paths. The difference is the point — in one you never touch a card, in another you pick every one of them.

Vedic

  1. Your birth details, once. Date, place, and how sure you are of the time — "I don't know" is a real answer and she works within it.
  2. You ask. In English or Hinglish, about a stretch of your life rather than a day.
  3. She reads your chart. The period running now, the one after it, and what today's transits are doing to your own houses.

No cards here. This one is worked out from your chart.

Tarot

  1. You ask. One straight question.
  2. You pick. The deck is shuffled in front of you and you choose — one card, or five for the full shape of it. Ru draws only if you ask her to, and the screen says which of you chose.
  3. She reads what you picked, position by position: Past, Now, Block, Advice, Ahead. Ask her to clarify any one of them.

You choose the cards. That was a decision, not an accident.

Oracle

  1. You come as you are. No question needed — this is for the days you cannot word it yet.
  2. One card, drawn with a breath. Its own deck, not the tarot in another coat.
  3. One short message. No spread, no positions, nothing to decode.

The shortest of the three, on purpose.

Plain words, on purpose

Jargon is how a reading avoids being checked. If you cannot act on a sentence, it has not told you anything.

What you usually get

“Shani is transiting your 8th house in the Vimshottari Mahadasha of Rahu, with Mars aspecting the lagna lord.”

What Ru says

“The next few months will feel slow and that is not your doing — it is a stretch where things take longer than they should. Start what you can finish. The pace picks up again from March.”
